# Audio attributions

Generated by hand from `manifest.json` - regenerate this by eye whenever the
manifest changes, since there's no automation for it yet and a stale credit
list is worse than none. If that gap gets annoying, a `tools/vendor-audio.mjs
--attributions` mode that renders this file from the manifest is the fix; not
built because the manifest is still small enough to reconcile by reading it.

None of this is DragonRealms audio. Simutronics owns their game's sound and
we don't have a license to it - everything here is sourced separately and
recorded below with where it came from and what it costs to use.
