# The Crossing Guild Dossiers

Status: **mandatory civic special list**

Elanthipedia lists ten physical guild halls in The Crossing: Barbarian, Bard,
Cleric, Empath, Moon Mage, Paladin, Ranger, Thief, Trader, and Warrior Mage.
They are ten independent special destinations, not palette variations of one
generic hall. [Crossing civic index](https://elanthipedia.play.net/Crossing)

| Dossier | Map scope | Must be distinct |
|---|---:|---|
| Barbarian | zone 1, 20 named guild cells | granite hall, arena, training, dormitory |
| Bard | zone 1, 12 named guild cells | arts-house, commons, performance/backstage |
| Cleric | zone 1, 19 named guild cells | cloister, chapel, contemplative rooms |
| Empath | zone 1, 5 named guild cells plus Healerie portals | healing garden, clinical rooms, underground haven |
| Moon Mage | zones 8/8a, 77 Observatory cells | massive observatory, vertical celestial rooms/gardens |
| Paladin | zone 1, 39 named guild cells | civic campus, courtyards, stable, jousting |
| Ranger | zone 1, 7 named guild cells | maintained grove and tree houses |
| Thief | zone 1, 13 named guild cells | concealed access and non-leaking interior |
| Trader | zone 1, 24 named guild cells | modest facade, opulent auction/mercantile interior |
| Warrior Mage | zone 7, 20 named guild cells | marble tower, pink arches, elemental chambers |
| Necromancer | no verified public Crossing hall | unresolved secret record only |

## Barbarian

Create a broad granite/black-steel training compound around the Main Hall,
Armory, Stadium Pits, Champions' Arena, stands, atrium, recovery rooms and
dormitory row. The source-backed Main Hall has towering granite walls,
polished mahogany, high banner space, carpets and a raised podium; build the
arena as a separate playable table. Do not bake combatants, weapons, gore,
crowds, trophies, named crests or shop stock. [Main Hall evidence](https://elanthipedia.play.net/Barbarians_of_renown)

## Bard

Create a north-central arts house east of Oxenwaithe Bridge: restrained street
frontage outside, ruby-carpet/gold-laced-red-velvet Commons inside, then a
performance floor, backstage stair, repair room, prop shell, conservatory and
balcony. The performance room retains open player space; instruments, actors,
audience, notation, curtains and readable signage remain live/approved props.
[Bard room evidence](https://elanthipedia.play.net/Bard_Guildhall_%28Crossing%29)

## Cleric

Create a calm, human-scale guild cloister distinct from the High Temple:
Sanctorum, study, library, courtyard, gathering hall, refectory, chapel and
residential cloisters. Use dark-brown coarse-grained wood for the circular
Sanctorum and a small steep-roof chapel with blank altar/window slots. No deity
icons, scripture, congregation, relic or funeral tableau belongs in base art.
[Sanctorum](https://elanthipedia.play.net/Clerics_of_renown), [chapel](https://elanthipedia.play.net/Pilgrim%27s_badge)

## Empath

Create three linked families: cedar-arbor courtyard with fish fountain, willow,
benches and swing; pearl/cream-tile and white-marble infirmary; dark-mahogany
office; and a separate mossy underground Healerie portal sequence. Do not bake
patients, blood, supplies, food, named statues or secret-tunnel routes. [Full
Crossing Empath room evidence](https://elanthipedia.play.net/Crossing_Empath_Guild)

## Moon Mage

Create a massive out-of-city Observatory with a readable vertical stack of
first/second/third levels, foyer, library, gallery/reception/seers' rooms and
celestial gardens. The top-level treatment is a crystal hemisphere over a mat
floor with a material slot for sanctioned sky data, not a baked starfield or
generic wizard tower. [Guildhall scope](https://elanthipedia.play.net/Moon_Mage_Guildhall), [Third Level evidence](https://elanthipedia.play.net/Moon_Mages_of_renown)

## Paladin

Create a large ordered campus at Herald Street: formal threshold, skylit meeting
hall, library/armory/chambers, courtyards, training grounds, stable/yard/hayloft,
jousting arena, barracks and promenade. Horses, guards, lances, banners,
crests and tournaments are live/reviewed content; no single shiny “holy knight”
building may replace the campus. [Paladin Guildhall scope](https://elanthipedia.play.net/Paladin_Guildhall_%28Crossing%29)

## Ranger

Create a maintained grove/tree-house complex: main hall shelter, storeroom,
tree grove, west grove, vertical tree-house platforms and clear walkway/stair
anchors. It is a civic outdoor guild, not a wild forest, elven palace, hunting
camp or texture wall. Trees and platforms must preserve sight lines and legal
movement anchors.

## Thief

Create no visible Thieves' Guild landmark. The public-facing facade stays a
neutral Crossing building; the graph controls any concealed threshold. Inside,
use compact halls, foyer, library, exhibition plinths, shop shell, alchemy
workroom, training floor, den and master’s-den family. Do not leak location with
skulls, traps, locks, stolen goods, symbolography or glowing secret doors.
[Access boundary](https://elanthipedia.play.net/Thief_new_player_guide)

## Trader

Create an intentionally modest west-Crossing facade that transitions through a
portal into marble-columned, prosperous auction/mercantile rooms: hall, auction
foyer/hall, library, banquet/gathering rooms, records, shipment center, offices,
gallery, vault and wholesale/cellar spaces. Prosperity comes from scale and
material, never baked piles of money, inventories, ledgers, merchant flags or
readable ceiling fresco. [Trader hall evidence](https://elanthipedia.play.net/Trader_Guildhall_%28Crossing%29)

## Warrior Mage

Create a rebuilt polished-gray-marble tower outside the Northeast Gate. Its
pink marble arch threshold, vertical landings, library/workshop/history room,
elemental chambers, target range and warrens are one vertical complex. Each
elemental chamber starts as neutral geometry plus a material/effect slot; never
bake permanent fire, lightning, spell effects, weapons, familiars or named
donor text. [Tower source](https://elanthipedia.play.net/Warrior_Mage_Guildhall_%28Crossing%29)

## Necromancer

Keep a global guild registry record with `status: unresolved-secret`. There is
no verified public Crossing guildhall in the cited city list. Do not make a
facade, map pin, visible entrance or scenic hint until a reviewed source room
and access policy are admitted.
